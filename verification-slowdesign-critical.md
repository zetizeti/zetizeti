# Three-pass verification — slow-design + critical/counterculture/attention lenses

Mirror of the IxD corpus verification (`verification-interaction-design.md`): three **independent**
adversarial agents, each reviewing both lens files with a different mandate. Run 23 May 2026.

- **Pass 1 — factual/citation** (data-validator): author/title/year accuracy, attribution, claim-vs-source.
- **Pass 2 — logical** (critical-debugger): contradictions, overclaims, **flip-test** on each tension, gotcha questions, cross-entry redundancy.
- **Pass 3 — philosophical/SDC** (academic-analyst): descriptive→evaluative collapse in `the_tension`/`body`, the verdict-constituted-field meta-problem, conceptual fidelity, copyright/expression.

## The convergent finding

All three passes pointed at the **same structural defect**, located precisely: these lenses are *critiques*, and the critical verdict tended to (a) appear **asserted in the tool's own voice inside `the_tension`** (Pass 3), and (b) be handed to the student as a **one-sided / gotcha `question`** even where the body was two-sided (Pass 2). The body's "counter" was often *named, not argued* (Pass 1/2). Loaded verbs ("overwrites", "quietly removes/disqualify", "smuggled in", "rightly", "lets the design off the hook") leaked the lean.

**The uniform fix-axis (applied):**
1. Push the critical claim into **attributed reported speech** in `the_tension` ("Feminist HCI argues…", "the attention-economy critique counters…", "On this reading…", "critics argue…").
2. **De-gotcha** every `questions_it_invites` so a defensible "no / nothing / it doesn't / shared" answer is admissible.
3. **Strip loaded verbs**; replace presupposing binaries with genuine discriminations.
4. **Argue the counter** — give the conventional pole a case where it wins.

## Standing notes (not "fixed" — flagged)

- **The verdict-constituted-field meta-problem (Pass 3).** Decolonial design, feminist HCI, dark-patterns ethics, attention-economy critique exist *to issue verdicts*. SDC cannot dissolve this — it can only **manage it per sentence**: relocate the verdict into attributed reported speech and supply an equal-weight counter. `pluriversal` is the most acute; the discipline holds wherever attribution is consistent across `the_tension` **and** `body`. This is now done, but it is a per-sentence discipline to preserve on any future edit, not a solved problem.
- **Cross-entry redundancy (Pass 2)** — three clusters overlap: "neutral/universal isn't neutral" (`restraint` + `whose-values` + `pluriversal`), "engagement is the wrong clock" (`made-to-be-lived-with` + `attention-as-extracted-resource` + `engagement-vs-agency`), "convenience hollows capacity" (`the-shortcut` + `convivial-tools`). Not de-duplicated — the lenses are *meant* to overlap on shared conventional vocabulary; FTS bm25 will surface the best match. Watch if retrieval feels repetitive in use.
- **Recent secondary citations** (Jing 2024, Koskinen 2016, Bentvelzen 2022, Croon 2022, Raihan 2025, Bombaerts 2023, Bhargava & Velasquez 2020, Docherty 2022, Valasek 2021, Monge Roffarello 2025, Pierce year, Dunne "Design for Debate" 2008) — Pass 1 couldn't confirm these from memory. They were **sourced via Consensus** (see `verification-consensus-backing.md`), so they are real; the anchors (Dunne, DiSalvo, Gaver, Bardzell, Escobar, Illich, Turner, Wu, Williams, Schüll, Hallnäs & Redström, Strauss & Fuad-Luke) are all confirmed correct.

All 18 lens entries are now **de-verdicted**: the critical claim is attributed, the conventional pole carries argued weight, and the question handed to the learner admits either answer.
