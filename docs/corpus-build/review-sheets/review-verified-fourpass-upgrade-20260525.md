## ADVISORY (9 flags across 7 entries)

### 1. `affordances-and-signifiers` · interaction-design.md · Pass 1
- **type:** attribution-precision. **defect:** "James Gibson coined *affordance* to mean the action possibilities the environment offers an organism, treated as **existing independent of the perceiver's experience**." — overstates a contested point; Gibson's own formulation is "equally a fact of the environment and a fact of behaviour… both… yet neither".
- **proposed fix:** "…the action possibilities the environment offers an organism — for Gibson, a fact of the environment that does not depend on the organism's *recognising* it" (keeps the Norman *perceived*-affordance contrast without the stronger claim). The entry's own cited McGrenere & Ho (2000) already covers the nuance.

### 2. `the-shortcut-skips-the-journey` · slow-design.md · Pass 2
- **type:** loaded-verb. **defect:** "…what is lost, or **rightly** saved…" and "…efficiency **quietly deletes** the experience…".
- **proposed fix:** drop "rightly" ("…what is lost, or saved, by giving it to them instantly?"); neutralise "quietly deletes" → "removes" / "can cost". The tension is genuinely two-sided already; this is lexical leak only.

### 3. `speculative-design-for-debate` · critical-counterculture-design.md · Pass 2
- **type:** loaded-verb + presupposition. **defect:** "…have you confused the two?" (presupposes confusion); failure_mode "quietly **smuggles in** a single inevitable future"; question "What future does this design **quietly treat as inevitable**?"
- **proposed fix:** "…presents a single future as inevitable"; question → "What future does this design treat as inevitable?"; soften the stem → "…which of those are you actually doing?"

### 4. `adversarial-design-and-agonism` · critical-counterculture-design.md · Pass 2
- **type:** presupposing questions. **defect:** "What conflict does this smoothness **hide**?" and "Whose disagreement **disappears** when this becomes seamless?" — both assume a conflict *is* being hidden; a learner whose smoothing legitimately serves the task can only answer by inventing one.
- **proposed fix:** "Is there a conflict this smoothness is resolving, or one it is hiding — and which is it here?" and "Does anyone's disagreement disappear when this becomes seamless, or is there genuinely nothing to disagree about?"

### 5. `convivial-tools-vs-dependence` · critical-counterculture-design.md · Pass 2 **and** Pass 3 (two flags, one entry — values-adjacent)
- **Pass 2 (counter named-not-argued):** the convivial side gets two named theorists (Illich + Turner) + a loss-narrative; the convenience pole gets one unattributed clause ("not everyone wants or needs to repair everything"). **proposed fix:** give convenience an argued mechanism — "convenience is not mere dependence — managed, hands-off tools widen who can use a thing at all (people without the time, expertise, or wish to maintain it), and the demand that everyone be a tinkerer is itself exclusionary."
- **Pass 3 (smuggled verdict):** "the counterculture-to-cyberculture lineage (Turner) **shows** how personal computing carried — **and then largely lost** — this emancipatory promise to commercial platforms" asserts a declension narrative as fact in the tool's voice. **proposed fix:** "…(Turner) **traces how** personal computing carried an emancipatory promise that, **on this reading, commercial platforms later narrowed**."
- **note:** this is the entry where the lens leans most toward zetizeti's own anti-platform values — worth your eye.

### 6. `engagement-vs-agency` · critical-counterculture-design.md · Pass 3 (values-adjacent)
- **type:** tool-voice / smuggled verdict. **defect:** "…structured analysis of major platforms **catalogues dozens of engagement-prolonging designs that pressure, entice, trap and lull users.**" — "trap" and "lull" are verdict-words stated as fact, pre-deciding the entry's own question.
- **proposed fix:** attribute the taxonomy — "…catalogues dozens of engagement-prolonging design patterns **its authors group as** pressuring, enticing, trapping and lulling users."

### 7. `dark-patterns-and-ethics` · interaction-design.md · Pass 3
- **type:** descriptive→evaluative slide (in a discipline-register entry). **defect:** "The live question **is not** whether a pattern lifts engagement but whether it preserves the user's agency, informed consent and an easy exit." — forecloses the question by fiat, removing standing from the commercial/affirmative pole (whereas `friction-and-frictionlessness` keeps "whose interest" open).
- **proposed fix:** "**For Brignull and Gray** the live question is not whether a pattern lifts engagement but whether it preserves the user's agency, informed consent and an easy exit — **though a designer who must also keep a product viable will weigh the engagement gain against that, not simply discount it.**"
