## Consensus validations (two-sidedness confirmed) — RE-RUN on Pro account (full 20-paper results)

These are the backing-log anchors for the four entries when drafted. All four confirmed **genuinely
two-sided with named evidence on BOTH poles** (Pro lifted the free-tier 3-result cap, surfacing the
quantitative anchors the first pass had hidden).

- **slow-fashion-or-the-livelihood** ✓✓ — *decelerate* pole: Niinimäki 2020 (*Nat. Rev. Earth & Env.*,
  explicit call to "decelerate manufacturing"), Peters 2021 (eliminate fast fashion as a business model),
  Bick 2018 (environmental-justice). *Livelihood* pole now has hard evidence: **Repp 2021** (*Resources,
  Conservation & Recycling* — circular/deceleration transition *reduces* apparel-production jobs in
  low/middle-income countries, shifts them to EU reuse/recycling), **Williams 2022** (concludes fast
  fashion "net positive"), **Barua 2025** (Bangladesh RMG workforce *displacement* from the slow-fashion
  shift), Wan 2021 (LMIC "progress at high social/environmental cost").
- **the-hide-or-the-plastic** ✓✓ *nuanced (three-way)* — da Silva 2026 (cradle-to-gate: bovine **highest**
  impact, fossil-PU **lowest**, but a "significant trade-off between impacts and durability"; excludes
  use/disposal), Hildebrandt 2020 (substitutes win **only** "coupled with less frequent replacement"),
  **Ding 2011** (hide better on human-health/resources, PU better on ecological-environment — a clean split),
  **D'Adamo 2024** (experts caution synthetics "may have greater drawbacks"), Tewari 2023 ("marketing
  gimmick?"). *Maturing third pole:* mycelium **Reishi (Williams 2022)** ≈ 8% of bovine footprint, <1%
  plastic — but unscaled/durability-unproven. Hinge = lifespan + by-product allocation + end-of-life, NOT
  "vegan is worse."
- **defensible-space-or-the-open-street** (drafted; slug renamed from `design-out-crime-or-the-fear-fortress` after Pass 2/3) ✓✓ — *works* pole: **Rupp 2025** (community-engaged CPTED →
  steeper violent/firearm-crime declines, β<0), Cozens 2005 ("growing body supports"), Armitage (burglary
  deterrence). *Inconclusive/displaces/fortress* pole: **Cohen 2014** ("yet to be definitively
  demonstrated… years of limited success"), Cozens 2005 ("inconclusive and much criticised"), **Cho 2018**
  (WDQ: cut assaults in-zone but **displaced** crime to adjacent areas), Sohn 2016 (permeability can *raise*
  crime), + Davis/Low fortress social-cost. *Nuance:* Kim 2019 (2nd-gen CPTED → benefit **diffusion**, not
  displacement). Actual-vs-perceived / reduce-vs-displace / safe-for-whom all hold.
- **the-compact-city-or-the-room-to-sprawl** ✓✓ *non-linear / size-contingent* — *pro-compact:* **Gudipudi
  2016** (doubling density → ≥42% CO₂ cut), **Güneralp 2017** (*PNAS* — dense development → less energy
  overall), Yi 2021 (holds for medium cities). *Contested/reversed:* **Zarco-Periñán 2021** (higher density →
  *higher* per-capita building emissions), Muñiz 2019 (density insignificant; income explains), Xu 2019
  (population-density vs physical-compactness push **opposite** ways), **Yin 2024** (inverted-U), **Xing
  2024** (in large/megacities compactness *increases* emissions). "Denser is greener" holds only within
  bounds and reverses at the extremes — ideal Socratic two-sidedness.
