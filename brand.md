# zetizeti — brand identity (23 May 2026)

**This is a standalone product, NOT a Koher product.** No Koher voice, palette, UI system, or domain. Its own identity. The visual identity is built in Prayas's own artist register (via the `frontend-design-prayas` skill) — vivid, flat, high-chroma, abstract.

## Name
**zetizeti** — a reduplication of **zeti**, clipped from *zetetic* (Greek *zeteo*, ζητέω: "to inquire, seek, question"). *Zetetic* means "proceeding by inquiry" — the Socratic act itself. The doubling is the point: catchy, globally pronounceable (ZEH-tee-ZEH-tee), in the reduplication family (bonbon, tuk-tuk, Chupa Chups); the meaning lives in sound, not transparency. Domain: **zetizeti.com** (registered 23 May 2026; supersedes aaraaya.net). (Naming rationale in `../docs/concept/spec.md`.)

**The interlocutor is "the stone"** — the voice persona inside zetizeti. A whetstone does not cut for you; it is the stone you draw your own edge against. The name is the inquiry; the stone is who asks.

## One line
**"It asks the questions. You do the thinking."**

## Longer
A questioning partner for design students. Name what you're trying to do; it asks back. It never tells you what to do — it grounds each question in the real, unresolved tensions of your discipline, and leaves the thinking to you.

## Voice
Spare, attentive, unhurried. Second person. Asks; does not assert. Never reassures, never concludes. The interlocutor is "the stone."

## Flow — goal-based
The opening is framed around the learner's **goal**, not a generic prompt: *"What are you trying to do right now — and where does it resist you?"* Each question that follows sharpens that goal. The register is **deep dialogue, not skimming** — wide measure, generous leading, unhurried pacing; the page invites reading, not scanning.

## Visual identity
- **Mood:** an abstract painting you think inside. Vivid, flat, high-chroma, matte — bold colour as the protagonist. Not SaaS, not dark-atmospheric, not muted.
- **Palette:** deep cobalt ground `#173a8c` · saturated **gold** `#f0b324`, **vermilion** `#e8431f`, **amber** `#ec8a1e` as the warm fields · **teal/verdigris** `#0f7d72`/`#0a4f49` (Oleomingus note) for the machinery · bone `#F6E8C6` text · dark blue-ink `#14224a` on the warm fields. High-chroma complementary contrast — warm against cobalt.
- **Composition:** large flat matte colour **fields** with abstract organic edges (after Prayas's `sd06`), asymmetric, bleeding off-edge — the page reads as an abstract painting. The learner's words sit in bone on the cobalt; the stone's questions are held in flat saturated colour fields, set big for reading.
- **Type:** *Rozha One* (Indian Type Foundry high-contrast didone) for the wordmark and the stone's questions — dramatic, voiced, not European-literary. *Besley* (warm slab) for the learner's words and reading text. *Space Mono* for the machinery shown behind the curtain. Two-voice split carried by typeface, not italics.
- **Mark:** an **abstract** jewel cluster (after `ib00`) — overlapping gold / teal / vermilion teardrops, flat and matte. It *gestures* at the honed edge; it does not depict a stone. (Earlier literal stone-and-spark and red-line-spine treatments were rejected.)
- **Motif:** flat saturated colour fields meeting at organic edges; one warm note (vermilion) struck against the cool ground. No glow, no blur, no atmospheric gradients — matte paint.

## Behind the curtain
A toggle reveals, beneath each question, the tensions the engine retrieved and the original works they cite — the grit the question was drawn against, shown on a flat verdigris field. The machinery is shown, never hidden.
